# OlaCheck – BMG Smart Retail Management System
 
## 📋 Overview
OlaCheck is an Information Systems Design project developed for a real-world client, aimed at building a smart retail management system for BMG. The project covered the full development lifecycle from business requirement analysis and system design to implementation, testing, and delivery carried out by a 5-member cross-functional team.
 
**Duration:** Jan 2026 – Apr 2026

**Team size:** 5 members

**Client:** BMG Trading & Logisitics JSC
 
## 👤 My Role: Project Manager
As Project Manager, I led the team throughout the full project lifecycle, coordinating across Business Analysis, Backend, Frontend, Testing, and Documentation roles.
 
**Key responsibilities:**
- **Project Leadership** – Directed a 5-member team through planning, requirements analysis, system design, and delivery
- **Client Coordination** – Worked directly with the client to understand business needs and align proposed system functionality with stakeholder expectations
- **Project Planning** – Defined project scope, deliverables, milestones, task allocation, and documentation processes
- **Task Management** – Managed the product backlog, sprint milestones, and daily task allocation across Analysis, Development, and Testing roles
- **Requirements Engineering** – Participated in business process analysis and requirement modeling using UML/BPMN
- **Stakeholder Communication** – Coordinated requirement clarification between business stakeholders and the technical team
- **Quality Assurance** – Supported system validation and testing to ensure delivered features aligned with agreed requirements
